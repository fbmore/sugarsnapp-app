Pilot

Card declines

Guest checkout is an anonymous page. A stranger can’t read anything and can’t pay themselves — but they can start card attempts against a vendor’s account, and because these are direct charges every decline lands on that vendor’s Stripe record. A run of them is what triggers a risk review or a payout hold, for a problem they didn’t cause and can’t see.

What a vendor can do about it: raise the minimum they accept by card (testers use the smallest amount that works), turn off custom amounts so only catalogue items can be bought, or take the shop page down for a day. Their own Stripe Radar rules are what actually block the attempts — this is the early warning that sends them there.
