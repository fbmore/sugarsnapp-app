How it works

01

Type an amount

Giant calculator keys you can hit in gloves, in sunlight, in a rush. Multiply for price × quantity, add to combine lines. Or tap your items if you keep a catalog.

02

The screen flips to face your customer with the price locked. Brightness ramps up so the code scans in direct sun, then restores itself.

03

Card through Stripe, or Venmo and PayPal — the apps already on their phone. Cash and EBT log with one tap.

04

It lands in the ledger

Stripe and PayPal confirm themselves. Venmo confirms with one tap. Every rail collects in the same daily total, with CSV export for your records.

Fair questions

Do I need a card reader?

No. Customers scan a QR with the payment app already on their phone, or pay by card through Stripe’s hosted checkout. There is no dongle to buy, charge, or lose.

Where does the money go?

Straight to your own Stripe, PayPal, or Venmo account. SugarSnapp is never the merchant of record, never holds your funds, and takes no cut of any sale.

Does it work in a dead zone?

The register and QR codes work offline. Confirming electronic payments needs signal — where there is none, take cash or Venmo and log it with one tap. Offline sales sync when the signal returns.

I’m on Android. Can I still use it?

Yes, through the browser. Sign in to your checkout page from any phone’s browser and ring up sales there — same catalog, same ledger. The native app is iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

What about EBT?

EBT sales are logged in your ledger for bookkeeping, not processed. SugarSnapp does not process EBT or SNAP payments and claims no FNS certification.

Whose Stripe account is it?

Yours. Connecting from SugarSnapp creates a standard Stripe account in your name, and you get the full Stripe Dashboard — payments, payouts, disputes, fraud rules. We can create a charge on it when you ring up a sale, and nothing else. Stripe bills you their standard fee directly; SugarSnapp adds nothing on top.

What if a customer disputes a charge?

It’s between you, your customer, and Stripe. They’ll take the disputed amount from your Stripe balance while it’s investigated and email you a deadline; you respond in your own Stripe Dashboard. We aren’t the merchant of record, so we can’t contest it for you — but your ledger and the customer’s stamped receipt are usually the evidence you need, and both export.

Can I refund from the app?

Yes — from the row in your Ledger. Card sales are refunded on your Stripe account, out of your Stripe balance. Cash, Venmo, PayPal and EBT have nothing for us to reverse, so the ledger marks them refunded for your books and you hand the money back the way you took it.
