Getting paid

How do customers pay?

Type an amount and hit Charge. The screen flips to face your customer with a QR code for each payment app you've connected. They scan with their phone camera, the amount is pre-filled, and they pay in their own app. Stripe and PayPal sales confirm automatically; Venmo sales you confirm with one tap when the notification arrives.

Connecting Stripe

Settings → Get paid → Connect next to Stripe. One button, no API keys — you sign in on Stripe's own page (or create a Stripe account there). Money goes straight to your Stripe account; SugarSnapp takes no cut.

Connecting PayPal or Venmo

Enter your PayPal.Me name or your Venmo business profile handle in Settings. Venmo requires a business profile for selling.

Cash and EBT

Tap Cash or EBT on the charge screen to log the sale instantly. EBT is recorded in your ledger for complete bookkeeping — SugarSnapp does not process EBT/SNAP payments.

Common issues

The QR won't scan in bright sun

SugarSnapp automatically boosts brightness during checkout. If it's still hard to scan, check Settings → Display & Brightness on your device and make sure auto-brightness isn't capped by Low Power Mode.

A sale shows "in progress" but the customer says they paid

"In progress" means the customer scanned and was sent to their payment app. Stripe and PayPal confirmations arrive over the network within seconds; on weak signal they can lag. If the customer shows you their confirmation, you can safely log the sale manually — it will carry a small self-confirmed marker so your ledger stays honest.

No signal at my market

The register, cash logging, and manual confirmation all work offline. QR payments need a connection (yours and the customer's) to confirm. Sales logged offline sync when you're back on signal.

Your data

Exporting sales

Ledger tab → share icon exports the visible day as CSV through the standard iOS share sheet.

Sending a receipt

Open any sale in the Ledger and tap "Send receipt" — it composes into your own Messages or Mail. Works for cash sales too.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account removes your account and all synced data permanently. Your money and records inside Stripe/PayPal/Venmo are unaffected. See the privacy policy for details.
