How the tent runs

01

Log the swipe

A shopper converts EBT at your tent. Record it once and the app mints the token batch for you — SNAP in $5 and $1, match in $2.

02

Vendors check in

Each market gets a six-character join code. Vendors type it, scan your printed sign, or tap a link. Check-in resets each market day.

03

Rules tracked for you

SNAP buys food, not crafts or flowers. Match is produce only. One match per card per day, up to your market’s cap. The app watches all three.

04

Reconcile in minutes

Issued at the tent against redeemed at stalls, per vendor. When the day squares it says so; when it doesn’t, the delta points at a stall.

Built to survive an audit

Token programs get reported on. The tent keeps records that hold up when someone asks.

✓ Append-only audit trail

Every swipe, spend, edit, and override is appended with an actor, a timestamp, and a required reason. Nothing in the app edits or prunes the log — it survives resets, because it is the evidence.

✓ The physical count wins

Count every vendor’s handed-in tokens at the end of the day, including vendors who logged sales in the app. Where the two disagree, the count is the record.

✓ Per-card match caps

Only the last four digits of a card are kept, and only to enforce the daily match cap. Full card data is never captured or stored.

✓ A grant report that prints

Daily summary — SNAP total, match total, tokens redeemed, vendors redeeming — as CSV and a one-page PDF, with the disclaimer that SugarSnapp records data and does not certify eligibility.

✓ Worth a look, never blocked

Unusually large redemptions and eligibility overrides get surfaced for your eye. The app flags them; it never silently removes or rejects anything.

✓ Vendors keep their privacy

Organizers never see a vendor’s sales, ledger, or payment connections — only what they redeemed at your market. That’s the sentence that lets you recruit without a trust fight.

Fair questions

Does SugarSnapp process EBT?

No. The tent records EBT swipes you have already run on your own terminal, so the tokens you issue can be tracked and reconciled. SugarSnapp does not process EBT or SNAP payments and claims no FNS certification.

What does it cost the market?

Nothing. Organizer accounts are free — no subscription, no payment method, no per-token fee.

How do vendors join my market?

With a six-character join code, which you can share as a link, a printed QR sign, or a PDF from the check-in kit. Checking in binds that day’s sales to your market; it resets each market day and vendors can undo it.

Do my vendors have to use SugarSnapp?

No. Count their tokens by hand as you do now — the physical count is always the record. Vendors who do use it simply mean fewer numbers to chase at the end of the day.

Where does the tent’s data live?

On your device first, backed up to your account. You can view the tent from any browser after the market, though that view is read-only — changes happen in the app.
