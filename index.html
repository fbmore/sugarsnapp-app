For market stalls, craft fairs & pop-ups

Type an amount.
Show a QR. Get paid.

SugarSnapp is a dead-simple register for market vendors. Customers scan the payment app they already use, and every sale — card, PayPal, Venmo, cash, or EBT — lands in one clean daily ledger.

Coming soon to the App Store

A register, not a system

Big calculator keys, price × quantity, lines that add up. No inventory setup, no account needed to start.

The charge flip

Hit charge and the screen flips to face your customer — price locked, one QR per payment app, brightness boosted for sunlight.

One honest ledger

Stripe, PayPal, Venmo, cash, and EBT collect in the same daily total, with CSV export for your records.

Your money stays yours

Payments go straight to your own accounts. SugarSnapp never touches the money and takes no cut of any sale.

Straight from the app

Built for the stall, not the back office

✓ Quick Charge

Type an amount and charge it. Multiply for quantities, add for baskets. A live receipt builds as you type.

✓ Scan-ready in sunlight

Brightness ramps up just for the scan and restores itself after. High-contrast QR codes, even in dark mode.

✓ Every payment type

Stripe and PayPal confirm automatically. Venmo confirms with one tap. Cash and EBT log with one tap — first-class, no apology.

✓ No hardware

No card reader, no dongles. Customers use the payment app already on their phone.

✓ Receipts when asked

Any sale can become a polished receipt, sent from your own Messages or Mail — even cash sales.

✓ Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. Your sales data belongs to you, and you can delete your account in-app any time.
