✓ Quick Charge

Type an amount and charge it. Multiply for quantities, add for baskets. A live receipt builds as you type.

✓ Scan-ready in sunlight

Brightness ramps up just for the scan and restores itself after. High-contrast QR codes, even in dark mode.

✓ Every payment type

Stripe and PayPal confirm automatically. Venmo confirms with one tap. Cash and EBT log with one tap — first-class, no apology.

✓ No hardware

No card reader, no dongles. Customers use the payment app already on their phone.

✓ Receipts when asked

Any sale can become a polished receipt, sent from your own Messages or Mail — even cash sales.

✓ Private by design

No ads, no data sold, and no advertising or cross-site tracking. We record a handful of milestones on our own servers — whether a first sale happened, which payment rails get used — and never anything about your customers. Fair questions

Do my customers need the app?

No. Customers pay with the payment app already on their phone, or — if you enable self-checkout — right in their browser. Nobody installs anything, nobody makes an account.

What does it cost me?

SugarSnapp takes no cut of any sale, ever. Payments settle to your own accounts; card payments cost only your processor’s standard fee. The app is free under $300 a month in tracked sales; past that, a founding subscription is $10/month flat.

Does it work in a dead zone?

The register and QR codes work offline. Confirming electronic payments needs signal — where there is none, take cash or Venmo and log it with one tap. The honest answer is the one your market already knows.
