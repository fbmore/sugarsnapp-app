1. The service

SugarSnapp is an iOS app and companion web checkout by Francesco Bertocci LLC ("we") that provides a sales register, payment QR codes, an optional self-checkout page for your stall, market-organizer tools, and a ledger for independent vendors. SugarSnapp is currently free during early access. Planned pricing is: free up to $500 of monthly sales volume, then a flat $5/month. We reserve the right to modify pricing and introduce new tiers with reasonable notice; existing subscribers will be notified before their next renewal, and founding subscribers keep their founding price.

2. Not a payment processor, not a party to your sales

SugarSnapp does not process, hold, or transmit funds, and is never a party to a sale. Payments occur directly between you and your customers through your own Stripe, PayPal, or Venmo accounts, under those services' terms. That includes card payments made through your self-checkout page and contactless card-present payments where available: those are processed by Stripe on your own Stripe account, under your agreement with Stripe, and the only fee is Stripe's — SugarSnapp adds no per-transaction fee and never holds funds. We are never the merchant of record and we take no commission. Where the app offers a refund action, it instructs your payment provider to refund from your balance on your behalf — we never refund from our own funds, and for cash, Venmo, PayPal and EBT the ledger simply records the refund you made yourself. Disputes and chargebacks are strictly between you, your customer, and your payment provider: the disputed amount comes from your balance, you respond through your provider’s dashboard, and SugarSnapp provides records (receipts, ledger) but does not mediate and cannot contest a dispute for you. EBT sales are recorded for bookkeeping only; SugarSnapp does not process EBT/SNAP payments.

3. Your self-checkout page

Enabling the public checkout page is optional and off by default. If you enable it:

  • Your stand name, logo, address, website, and item catalog become publicly visible on that page — that is the feature. Your contact email and phone number are not exposed.
  • You are responsible for keeping item names and prices accurate, and for fulfilling orders that customers have paid for. A customer's paid receipt (the receipt page showing a PAID stamp and timestamp) is their proof of payment — honor it.
  • Disable the page when you are not selling; disabling takes it offline immediately. Re-enabling restores the same link.
